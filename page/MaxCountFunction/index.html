Description#

Attributes#

This tag has no attributes

See also#

   <c:if test="${mak:count()==1}">These are the list elements: </c:if>
   <tr bgcolor="${mak:count()%2==1?"white":"grey"}">
        <td>this shows in alternate colors</td>
        ...
   </tr>
   <c:if test="${mak:count()!=mak:maxCount()}">
